Oak chevron flooring

Angled board ends meet in continuous points. Explore 45° and 60° chevron configurations, also known as point de Hongrie.

Tailored quote

The two cuts create different pattern openings. Compare the product specifications and request a sample before choosing a grade or finish. Interior images illustrate the pattern and do not replace technical specifications.

Delivery throughout France. European projects considered on request.
