Oak herringbone flooring

Rectangular blocks meet at right angles with staggered ends. Explore herringbone flooring in a range of sizes and oak grades.

Tailored quote

Unlike chevron, square-ended herringbone blocks do not form a continuous mitered point. Discuss the layout, quantities and cutting allowance with your installer before ordering.

Delivery throughout France. European projects considered on request.
